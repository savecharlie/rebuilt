# Getting the two inputs

Neither is redistributed here; both are one download.

**GCMT, 1976–2020** — `jan76_dec20.ndk` (23 MB), from
https://www.ldeo.columbia.edu/~gcmt/projects/CMT/catalog/jan76_dec20.ndk
`parse.py` reads it and validates itself against the published scalar moments of the 2004
Sumatra-Andaman and 2011 Tohoku solutions before anything else runs.

**Global Heat Flow Database, Release 2024 v.2026.03** (only needed for the two failed
instruments) — https://doi.org/10.5880/fidgeo.2024.014 → `GHFBD-R2024_v.2026-03.zip`, unzip to
`ghfdb/`. CC-BY-4.0.
